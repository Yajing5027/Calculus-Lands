Dear Adventurer Sigma,

Our Calculus Continent is filled with many illusions. Adventurer Sigma, on the path to truth, there are illusions that look very familiar. They pretend to be convergent series, perfect elementary functions, or simple integrals. If you act without thinking, the abyss will swallow you. Try to let your collected intuition guide you. The very first second you see a series or an integral, your intuition will tell you which "move" to use. But intuition can also lie to you. Many times, you think you should use one method, but you are actually falling into a trap.

You will travel through the five theme lands of Calculus II. In each land, the Chaos Knight Limitus has set "intuition traps." After you defeat the small soldiers, the lord of the land, Owlculus, will give you the hardest puzzle. In every battle, you have many moves to choose from. Some are very powerful, some are just okay, and some do not work at all. This is a great chance to train.

Remember: There are no complex calculations here. Your weapon is your intuition, but your victory comes from seeing which "intuition" is a lie in a single moment. Choose your move and face the infinity. Do not be afraid to make mistakes. I will be with you to review every battle until your intuition is as sharp as a blade and cuts through all illusions.

At the end of the journey, you will no longer be fooled by the surface. You will become the master of your intuition. Good luck!

Dr. H
